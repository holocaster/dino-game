## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: visual target for a small Godot 3D dinosaur exploration game for children aged 3 to 5.
Create one landscape 16:9 image that convincingly looks like a polished, achievable real-time cartoon 3D game scene, with an original juvenile triceratops as the playable character in a sunny forest clearing. Show the proposed character and environment together in one coherent full-screen game view.
The triceratops has a compact sturdy terracotta-orange body, a large rounded head, warm expressive dark eyes, a pale cream muzzle and belly, three very short blunt rounded horns, a gently scalloped orange frill with subtle warm yellow accents, four clearly articulated short legs and a balanced tapering tail. Friendly curious expression with closed smiling mouth. All four legs must be anatomically distinct and readable. Use a three-quarter side view, head turned slightly toward the viewer so the face is visible. The main dinosaur fills approximately one third of the image height, fully visible in the foreground, and is walking naturally along a gently curving sandy trail.
Environment: continuous organic grassy terrain filling the image, a clear shallow turquoise pond with rounded stone edges, clusters of ferns and broad leaves, rounded-canopy trees with varied trunk shapes, small colorful flowers and a few butterflies. Use layered foreground, middle ground and background vegetation while keeping the trail and character easy to read. The trail curves toward an inviting open clearing. A few footprints in the trail give a visual exploration cue.
Camera: moderately elevated close third-person game camera with stable oblique angle, showing the full dinosaur and enough of the path ahead. The scene should feel like a place the player is inside, not a distant tabletop model. Keep the dinosaur silhouette readable against green foliage and avoid any plant hiding its face or feet.
Style: high-quality original cartoon 3D art with rounded but well-defined modeled forms, simple matte materials and clean color blocks, broad readable foliage, soft warm sunlight, soft contact shadows and restrained ambient occlusion. Lively fresh greens, warm sandy paths, blue-green water, small coral and yellow flower accents. Achievable moderate-detail mobile-web game art, no tiny surface detail, no expensive cinematic rendering.
No UI, no text, no logo, no watermark, no collage, no character sheet, no image borders. No square floating island, no isolated diorama platform, no plain surrounding void. No photorealistic scales, no horror, no weapons, no combat, no injuries, no sharp threatening horns or teeth. Do not reuse the previous thin long-neck dinosaur design.
